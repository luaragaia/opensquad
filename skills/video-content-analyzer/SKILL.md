# Video Content Analyzer

## When to use

After picking viral references in `instagram-research`, to turn "this worked" into a **template**:
a reusable hook formula and video arc the script writer can fill in the brand's voice.

## Workflow

1. **Collect the reference reels** (Instagram, TikTok or Shorts) chosen in the research step.
2. **Analyze each** — extract:
   - **Hook** (first 0–3s): the promise or tension that stops the scroll.
   - **Structure**: development, retention beats, and the CTA.
   - **Patterns**: pacing, on-screen text, format (talking head, b-roll, screen-record).
3. **Consolidate** into one pattern: hook formula + video arc + CTA type.
4. **Deliver a script skeleton** to the script step.

## Best practices

- Judge the hook first — most retention is won or lost in the first 3 seconds.
- Separate what is *replicable* (structure) from what is *account-specific* (a face, a meme).
- Keep the skeleton short — it's a scaffold, not a finished script.
